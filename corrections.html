Corrections

Help us keep the research trail accurate.

How to report an error

Send the page URL, the exact claim, what you believe is wrong, and a source that supports the correction to research@reincarnatedai.com.

What we review

We review factual errors, broken or misattributed links, incorrect dates, score inconsistencies, unsupported certainty, and missing material counterarguments. A difference in interpretation is useful feedback but is not automatically a correction.

What happens next

Material corrections are reviewed against the source record, applied to the relevant page, and dated in the page's review history. When an error changes the meaning of a case or conclusion, we add a short correction note rather than silently rewriting the record.
