Privacy

A plain-language account of what stays in your browser and what leaves it.

Local journal

The Experience Journal stores entries in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to ReincarnatedAI by the journal itself. Clearing browser storage, using private browsing, or browser policies can remove them. Export a JSON backup if an entry matters.

AI conversations

When you use RAI, your question and the limited conversation context needed for the response are sent to the AI service configured for the project. Do not include names, medical details, credentials, or other sensitive information you would not want processed by that service.

Forms

Contact and experience forms send the fields you submit through the site's email-delivery service for private review. Experience submissions require explicit consent and may be referenced anonymously in research synthesis. We do not publish identifying details without separate permission.

Cookies and analytics

The site currently uses browser storage for theme, bookmarks, journal entries, and chat restoration. No third-party advertising tracker is intentionally installed. A privacy-preserving analytics system may be added only with a clear update to this page.

Questions

For a privacy question or deletion request, email research@reincarnatedai.com and include the relevant submission details without sending the underlying account unless necessary.
